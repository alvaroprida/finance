Introduction

This page is a daily briefing on financial markets. It follows US equity indices, crypto, metals, the euro-dollar exchange rate and US Treasury yields, and each day it describes where every product stands in its cycle: its trend, momentum, distance from recent highs and how volatile it has been.

Read it top to bottom. First comes the cycle status of each market and the key observations of the day. Then there is one section per product group with a chart, the latest numbers and written analysis. The last sections show how the markets move together and which alert thresholds were crossed.

Cycle status describes the current state of each market using trend (price against its 50- and 200-day averages), not a forecast of what comes next.
